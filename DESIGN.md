# Repbook visual direction

Repbook is a quiet training journal translated from a paper workout log. The direction is athletic minimal: strong type, ruled dividers, one useful surface at a time, and muted red reserved strictly for primary action.

## Composition

- Today leads with the next session and one large Start workout action.
- Lists use dividers and whitespace instead of nested cards.
- Workout mode centers the current movement, reps, and Done action.
- Progress uses a restrained weekly bar record and a short list of meaningful history.

## Material and type

- Background and surfaces: Warm White `#F6F5F2`.
- Display voice: serif for journal-like exercise and session titles.
- Body voice: platform sans for legibility and quick scanning.
- Black `#202020` carries type and structural emphasis. Muted Red `#B9584F` marks primary actions plus important active, current, and progress states; never use vivid red. Success remains olive `#687560`.

## Quality bar

Each screen should answer its primary task within one second, keep controls at or above native touch target sizes, and remain calm during a tiring workout. Decoration must never compete with the next set.
